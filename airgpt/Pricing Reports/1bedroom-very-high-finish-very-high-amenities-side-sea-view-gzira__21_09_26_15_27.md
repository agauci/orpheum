Sequential calculation: raw final net = max(90, base net × (1 − duration discount) × (1 − last-minute discount)). Suggested final net = max(90, 5 × round(raw final net / 5)). The final €5 rounding makes effective discounts approximate. Ensure the booking platform's actual payout respects €90 even if its discount engine uses cents. No other promotion may stack without recalculating the floor.
